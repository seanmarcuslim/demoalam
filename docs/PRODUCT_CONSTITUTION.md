# DemoAlam Product Constitution v1

## Vision

DemoAlam is a decision-support platform that helps Filipinos make safer, clearer next moves in confusing real-life situations.

## Mission

Reduce the time, money, stress, and mistakes lost to uncertainty.

## Core Rule

DemoAlam is not for interesting information.

DemoAlam is for moments where a clearer next step can prevent damage.

Every future guide, feature, category, bundle, search improvement, and roadmap item must answer:

> Does this help a Filipino user make a safer, clearer next move?

If the answer is weak, forced, or vague, the idea should be rejected or reshaped.

## Product Principles

### 1. Next Step First

Every guide, feature, search result, bundle, and screen must help the user answer:

> Ano ang uunahin ko?

If the product explains but does not help the user act, it is incomplete.

### 2. Reduce Real Damage

DemoAlam should prioritize problems that can cost users money, time, safety, documents, access, dignity, job opportunity, government assistance, or emotional stress.

Low-impact trivia does not belong.

### 3. Calm Before Action

The app must reduce panic, not amplify it.

The tone should be calm, practical, non-judgmental, anti-shame, anti-hype, anti-fixer, and anti-scam.

DemoAlam should never push users into rash decisions.

### 4. Evidence Before Escalation

For work, money, scams, complaints, agencies, and legal-adjacent problems, DemoAlam should teach users to:

1. Clarify the issue.
2. Collect proof.
3. Ask through proper channels.
4. Escalate only when needed.

Do not train users to complain first without understanding the facts.

### 5. Trust Must Be Visible

Trust should not be hidden.

High-risk guides should clearly show official sources, limits, safe reporting channels, and warnings against fake helpers or fixers.

Future trust improvements should include reviewed dates, guide freshness, source confidence, and clear "not an official government app" transparency.

### 6. Search Is the Front Door

Most users will not browse categories when stressed.

They will search phrases like:

- kulang sahod
- wrong send gcash
- nawala atm
- loan app harassment
- valid id
- pcso medical assistance

Search quality is product quality. A high-ranking guide must also be a high-quality guide.

### 7. Fewer, Better Guides

DemoAlam should not chase guide count blindly.

A smaller set of specific, trusted, complete guides is better than a large library of thin content.

Add guides only when they solve real uncertainty.

### 8. Specific Beats Broad

Avoid broad content like:

- How to manage money
- Know your rights
- Government programs in the Philippines

Prefer specific situations:

- What to do if your salary is short
- Checklist before borrowing from a loan app
- What to prepare before asking for medical assistance
- What to do if your SIM with OTP is lost

Specific situations create action.

### 9. Filipino Reality Over Perfect Theory

DemoAlam should reflect how things actually work for ordinary Filipinos:

- long lines
- missing requirements
- inconsistent offices
- unclear agency processes
- family pressure
- cash shortage
- poor internet
- fear of authority
- scam exposure
- Taglish search behavior

The app should be practical, not idealistic.

### 10. Never Replace Official Channels

DemoAlam should guide users toward safer decisions, but it must not pretend to be a government agency, lawyer, bank, doctor, social worker, or official customer support.

It explains, prepares, and routes. It does not guarantee outcomes.

### 11. Uncertainty Must Be Real

DemoAlam should solve actual uncertainty, not imagined uncertainty.

Before creating a guide, ask:

> Do real Filipinos repeatedly encounter this problem, or did we simply think it sounded useful?

Preferred evidence sources:

- search analytics
- user interviews
- search failures
- government inquiry trends
- support requests
- observed behavior

Founder intuition alone is not enough.

## Acceptance Test

Before adding any guide, feature, bundle, category, search rule, or roadmap item, answer:

1. What uncertainty does this solve?
2. What damage does this prevent?
3. What decision becomes easier?
4. What safer next step becomes possible?
5. Is this specific enough?
6. Is this high-friction in real Filipino life?
7. Can it be supported with reliable sources?
8. Could wrong guidance harm users?
9. Would a stressed user understand it quickly?
10. Would this reduce the need to ask random Facebook comments?

If these answers are weak, do not build it yet.

## Rejection Test

Reject or postpone anything that is:

- merely interesting
- generic knowledge
- not tied to a decision
- not tied to real-world friction
- not searchable by a stressed user
- not supported by trustworthy sources
- likely to become outdated quickly without maintenance
- designed mainly to increase guide count
- designed only because other apps have it
- emotionally exciting but strategically weak
- too broad to produce a clear next step

## Strong Fit Examples

Strong DemoAlam problems usually sound like:

- What should I do first?
- What should I prepare?
- What should I avoid?
- Who should I contact?
- What proof do I need?
- What is the safer next step?

Examples:

- wrong-send GCash
- lost ATM or debit card
- lost SIM with OTP access
- stolen phone first hour
- kulang sahod
- loan app harassment
- valid ID confusion
- National ID problems
- PCSO or DSWD assistance preparation
- barangay complaint evidence

## Automatic Rejection Examples

Reject proposals like:

- motivational articles
- generic productivity advice
- general lifestyle content
- entertainment content
- broad political commentary
- general legal education without safe next steps
- general medical advice beyond safe routing and official sources
- broad financial education without a decision moment

DemoAlam should not become a general Filipino wiki.

## Strategic Direction

DemoAlam should become less like a library and more like a calm decision-support system.

Near term:

- trusted search
- stronger top guides
- validation with real users
- guide freshness
- saved and offline urgent guides

Medium term:

- guided flows
- decision trees
- personalized checklists
- emergency mode
- better analytics

Decision flows must follow DECISION_FLOW_CONSTITUTION.md.

Long term:

- AI-assisted routing
- local or context-aware recommendations
- trusted civic utility layer

The soul must stay the same:

> Help Filipinos know what to do next.
