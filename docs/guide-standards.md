# DemoAlam Guide Standards

DemoAlam guides must help ordinary Filipinos solve real problems calmly, safely, and practically while reducing panic, misinformation, scam risk, and fixer dependence.

## Purpose

Every guide should act like a calm decision helper, not a blog post. The user should quickly understand what matters first, what to prepare, what to avoid, and where to verify.

## Required Quality Bar

High-value guides should include:

- Clear, specific title
- Calm intro that names the real problem
- Immediate first actions for stressful situations
- Checklist users can follow
- Warning, scam, or fixer-risk section when relevant
- Practical steps in realistic order
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Realistic expectations about time, cost, eligibility, or limits
- Official sources when the guide involves government, money, IDs, health, school, jobs, or legal rights
- Sample questions, scripts, or messages when users may need to ask an office, school, bank, telco, LGU, or agency

## Writing Principles

- Write for stressed users with limited time.
- Prefer direct everyday language over formal agency language.
- Explain what to do first, not everything at once.
- Avoid hype, guarantees, or broad claims.
- Never imply approval, payout, eligibility, or legal outcome is guaranteed.
- Warn against fixers, fake links, OTP requests, processing fees, and unofficial registration forms.
- Make official verification feel normal, not intimidating.

## Structure Template

1. What to know
2. First actions
3. Priority order
4. Checklist
5. Common mistakes
6. Warning or scam/fixer risks
7. Step-by-step process
8. Sample questions or scripts
9. What to expect
10. Official sources

## Review Checklist

- Is the guide specific enough to solve one real problem?
- Does the first screen reduce panic?
- Can the user tell what to do in the first 5-30 minutes?
- Are checklists practical and not vague?
- Are warnings realistic for Filipino users?
- Are official sources present where needed?
- Does the guide avoid sounding like a government article or SEO blog?
- Would this help someone avoid wasted time, money, stress, or a fixer?
